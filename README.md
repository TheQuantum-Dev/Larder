# Larder

A pantry companion for students. Photograph what's in your fridge or cupboard and get a recipe you can make right now, with a rough sense of what it costs and how it fits your week. Built for students, by students!

## What it does

- Scan your pantry with your camera; on-device AI identifies what you have
- Get recipes ranked by how completely you can make them with what's on hand
- See a rough cost estimate per recipe and track a simple weekly food budget
- Cook Mode walks you through each recipe step by step
- Runs entirely on-device using Apple's Foundation Models framework — no cloud calls, no account required to try it

## Requirements

- iOS 26.5 or later
- An Apple Intelligence-capable device (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) for the on-device AI features
